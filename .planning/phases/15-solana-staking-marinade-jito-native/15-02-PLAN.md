<objective>
Ship the Marinade liquid-staking surface: `prepare_marinade_stake` (SOL-W-14 —
deposit SOL, mints mSOL) and `prepare_marinade_immediate_unstake` (SOL-W-15 —
mSOL → SOL now, with the VARIABLE fee surfaced verbatim).

Marinade IDL (D-01 path, mirrors Phase-13 MarginFi). NO new runtime dependency —
`@marinade.finance/marinade-ts-sdk` is the build-time IDL source only, never added
to `dependencies`.

trades a variable fee for instant liquidity — surfacing that fee verbatim is the
load-bearing user-protection requirement (SOL-W-15).

Output: `src/protocols/marinade.ts`, the vendored IDL, the on-chain fee-read helper,
two `prepare_marinade_*` tools, contracts SOT getters, the dispatch arm, and pinned
fingerprint fixtures I (deposit) + AA (liquidUnstake).
</objective>
