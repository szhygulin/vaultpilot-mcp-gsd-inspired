<!-- D-07 MarginFi — risk-weighted asset/liability (13-RESEARCH § Health Math) -->
  assets       = Σ (bankAssetQuantity_i × oraclePrice_i × assetWeight_i)
  liabilities  = Σ (bankLiabQuantity_j × oraclePrice_j × liabilityWeight_j)
  Solvent at maintenance when assets ≥ liabilities.
  health-factor-equivalent (SOL-W-03 surface) = assets / liabilities  (≥1 safe at the chosen margin type)
  Weights: use MAINTENANCE weights (assetWeightMaint / liabilityWeightMaint) for the liquidation-risk surface (Init weights gate NEW borrows — A3).
  riskTier: Isolated-tier assets get 0 asset-weight as collateral.

  Oracle staleness: banks carry oracleMaxAge + oracleMaxConfidence; for READS surface staleness rather than throwing (mirror Aave's userEModeCategoryId verbatim-surface caveat).
