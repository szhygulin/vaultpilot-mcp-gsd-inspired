<objective>
Build the two cryptographic-binding primitives of the BTC trust pipeline: per-input BIP-143/BIP-341 sighash computation (`src/signing/btc-sighash.ts`) and the domain-tagged keccak256 `payloadFingerprint` over the concatenated sighashes (`src/signing/btc-fingerprint.ts`). Output: `src/signing/btc-sighash.ts`, `src/signing/btc-fingerprint.ts`, `test/btc-sighash.test.ts`, Fixture O appended to `test/signing-fingerprint.test.ts`.
