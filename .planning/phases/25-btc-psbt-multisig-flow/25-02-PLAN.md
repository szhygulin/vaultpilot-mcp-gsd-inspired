<objective>
Add `combine_btc_psbts` — merge partially-signed PSBTs from multiple co-signers, with an explicit pre-combine conflict scan that refuses on same-key/same-input signature conflicts before `Psbt.combine()` is ever called.

Purpose: `bip174`'s combiner silently drops duplicate conflicting keys (self wins). Silent overwrite of a co-signer's signature is the load-bearing threat for this phase. The manual pre-scan is the mitigation.
Output: `combineBtcPsbts` helper in `btc-psbt.ts` + the `combine_btc_psbts` direct-transform tool (no handle, no payloadFingerprint).
</objective>
